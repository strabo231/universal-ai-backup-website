📋 1. OVERVIEW

ProTools ("we") respects your privacy. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information.

📊 2. WHAT DATA WE COLLECT

Data Stored Locally on Your Device:

  • License Validation Data: Email address (provided during purchase), License key (generated for your subscription), Subscription tier, Product type (which extension), Activation date, Validation timestamps
  • User Preferences:
    • Universal AI Backup: Export format preference, button position, settings

💡 Important: This data never leaves your device except during license validation. See "How We Use Your Data" section below.

Data We DO NOT Collect:

  • ❌ AI chat messages or responses (Universal AI Backup)
  • ❌ Tab content, titles, or URLs (Tab Manager Pro)
  • ❌ Exported file contents (either extension)
  • ❌ Browsing history
  • ❌ Behavioral analytics or tracking data
  • ❌ Location data
  • ❌ Device information beyond basic browser data

🔓 3. EXTENSION PERMISSIONS EXPLAINED

Universal AI Backup Permissions:

  • activeTab: Access the current tab to detect AI platforms and export conversations
  • storage: Save license status and user preferences locally
  • scripting: Inject export functionality into AI platform pages
  • tabs: Detect which AI platforms are open
  • contextMenus: Add right-click menu for quick export
  • host_permissions: Access specific AI platform websites (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, etc.) to enable backup functionality

Tab Manager Pro Permissions:

  • tabs: Read and manage browser tabs (core functionality)
  • storage: Save license status, user preferences, and saved sessions locally
  • activeTab: Highlight the currently active tab in the manager
Privacy Guarantee: Even with these permissions, we do NOT read tab content, track your activity, or send any browsing data to our servers.
